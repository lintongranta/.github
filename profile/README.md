For small businesses that have outgrown the spreadsheet but not the budget.

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| **Data infrastructure** | Sales figures in five spreadsheets, merged by hand every Monday | One table that updates itself at six every morning |
| **Analysis** | A monthly report that takes a day to build | A report that is ready when the month closes |
| **Automation** | Invoices re-keyed from email into the accounts | Invoices read and filed as they arrive |

And whatever else the problem turns out to be.

### How a job runs

`01` **Measure.** Find where the numbers live now, who touches them, and what goes wrong.

`02` **Build.** Make the smallest thing that fixes it, in the tools the business already uses where possible.

`03` **Hand over.** Documentation, a walkthrough, and code the business owns outright.
